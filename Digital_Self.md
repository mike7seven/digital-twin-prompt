# Digital Self

Whether you call it your Avatar, Virtual Persona or your Digital Self the world of Digital Twins is no longer science fiction, it's real, and it's being used to shape the future in ways most people don't even realize. 

Did you know that even human behavior are now being modeled in real time digital twins?

A "Digital Twin" is a virtual model of something in the real world an individual, a city, an economy, or even the entire planet. And yes even human behavior are now being modeled in real time digital twins.
